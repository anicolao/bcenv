# Vision for bcenv

bcenv exists to enable an AI agent to independently compete in the annual Battlecode competition.

We envision an agent that can take a new season's rules and materials, understand an unfamiliar game, develop its own strategies, and produce a competitive entry. It learns through experimentation, responds to failure, and makes decisions grounded in evidence. Humans set the objective and authorize participation; the agent carries out the intellectual and practical work of competing.

Independence means sustained ownership of that work. The agent can reason about competing approaches, write and refine its bot, judge its performance, and adapt without routine human coding, debugging, or strategic direction. It exercises that independence within the competition's rules and the time, resources, and permissions available to it.

bcenv makes this autonomy understandable. The agent's work leaves a faithful record of the instructions it received, the experiments it performed, and the evidence behind its decisions. People can inspect how an entry came to be, understand its limitations, and learn from both its successes and its failures.

The vision endures across annual changes to the game and advances in AI. bcenv gives agents a place to confront new competitive problems without depending on a single season, model, or predetermined strategy. Playing strength grows from the agent's ability to learn and adapt.

As a GPLv3 project, bcenv is an open foundation that others can study, modify, and build upon. It makes autonomous participation in Battlecode a shared field of experimentation, where the methods behind an entry can be as valuable as its competitive result.
